## Ingredients

- 1/2 lb Bacon
- 1 lb Ground beef
- 1 c diced onion
- 1/2 c Ketchup
- 1t salt
- 4T brown sugar
- 1t mustard
- 2t apple cider vinegar
- 1 med. can Pork & Beans
- 15.5 oz can Kidney beans
- 15.5 oz can Butterbeans

## Method

- Simmer 30 minutes

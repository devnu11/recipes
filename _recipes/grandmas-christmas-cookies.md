## Ingredients

- 1 pkg (10 oz) pitted fresh dates
- 1/2 cup Brazil nuts
- 1/2 cup filberts for almonds
- 1 cup pecans
- 1/3 cup candied pineapple
- 1/3 cup candied cherries
- 1/2 cup butter
- 1 cup sugar
- 1 egg
- 1 tsp salt
- 1/2 tsp cinnamon
- 1 cup sifted all purpose flour
- 1/2 tsp soda
- 3 tbl. fruit juice or water

## Method

- Cut dates in medium sixed pieces.
- Chop all nuts and pineapple coarsely.
- Cut cherries into quarters.
- Combine butter, sugar, unbeaten egg, salt and cinnamon and beat well.
- Sift flour with soda and blend into creamed mixture along with fruit juice.
- Add fruits and nuts and mix well.
- Drop rounded tablespoons onto lightly greased baking sheets 2 inches apart and bake at 350 deg. 15 to 18 minutes.
- Remove to wire rack to cool.

Yield: about 3 dozen

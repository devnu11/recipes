## Ingredients

- 1 cup plain low-fat yogurt
- 1/2 teaspoon each dill weed and Dijon mustard
- 1/2 cup chopped green bell pepper
- 1/2 cup thinly sliced green onions (including tops)
- 1 egg
- 1 tablespoon nonfat milk
- 1/3 cup fine dry bread crumbs
- 2 tablespoons grated Parmesan cheese
- 1 1/2 lb. fish fillets** (about 1-in. thick)
- Vegetable cooking spray

## Method

- Combine yogurt, dill weed, mustard, bell pepper and onions; stir until well combined; set aside.
- Place a large nonstick baking pan in a 500° oven.
- Beat egg and milk until blended;
- mix bread crumbs and cheese.
- Cut fish into serving-size portions.
- Dip fish first into egg mixture, then into crumb mixture, coating well on all sides.
- Spray fish on both sides with cooking spray.
- Remove pan from oven.
- Arrange fish in pan in a single layer;
- bake, uncovered until fish is golden brown and opaque in center when tested, about 10 minutes.
- Serve with yogurt sauce.

## Notes

6 servings.

**Suggested alternates: Rockfish, snapper, sea bass or cod.

Per serving: Calories 172; Protein 26g, 60%; Carbohydrates 8g, 19%; Fat 4g, 21%; Sodium 149mg.

## Ingredients

- 4 c fruit pureed
- 2 c sugar
- 1/4 c lemon juice
- 1 c evaporated milk
- 1/2 pt cream
- 2 t vanilla
- 1 egg

## Method

- + milk to 3/4 mark of freezer.
- Turn till frozen.

## Ingredients

- 4 oz Neufchâtel Cheese
- 1/2 cup skim milk
- 3 T sugar
- 1 egg yolk
- 1/2 t vanilla
- 1 qt strawberries

## Method

- In food processor or bowl combine all but vanilla & strawberries.
- Transfer mixture to 2 cup measure.
- Micro-cook uncovered on 50% power for 4-5 minutes, or till bubbly.
- Stir every minute.
- Stir in vanilla and chill at least 2 hours.
- Serve over berries

## Notes

Makes 6 servings

## Ingredients

- 2 Chicken breasts (cut in 1 inch pieces)
- 1 egg
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1/2 tsp sherry
- 1/2 tsp soy sauce
- flour
- 1/2 cup vinegar
- 2/3 cup sugar
- 1/4 tsp salt
- 1/3 cup catsup
- 4 drops tabasco sauce
- 1 tsp cornstarch
- 2 tbl water
- 1 cup pineapple chunks
- 1/2 cup green pepper (in chunks)

## Method

- Soak breasts in: 1 egg, 1/2 tsp salt, 1/2 tsp sherry, 1/2 tsp soy sauce
- Coat each piece lightly with flour.
- Deep fry until brown.
- Drain excess fat on towel.
- Drain oil from skillet in same pan add: 1/2 cup vinegar, 2/3 cup sugar, 1/4 tsp salt, 1/3 cup catsup, 4 drops tabasco sauce
- Cook at medium heat to boiling point, then thicken with cornstarch (mix 1 tsp cornstarch with 2 tbl water) until medium thick.
- Add: 1 cup pineapple chunks, 1/2 cup green pepper (in chunks) and chicken
- Toss and mix thoroughly until chicken absorbs sauce.

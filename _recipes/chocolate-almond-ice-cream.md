## Ingredients

- 2/3 c. slivered almonds - toasted
- 2 c. sugar
- 1/4 c. flour
- 2 lg eggs
- 2/3 c. unsweetened cocoa
- 3 c. half-&-half (light cream)
- 4 c. whipping cream
- 1 T vanilla

## Method

- In a 2-3 quart pan, combine sugar, flour & cocoa.
- Stir in eggs and half & half.
- Over medium heat, cook & stir until it comes to a gentle boil, about 12 min.
- Let mixture cool to lukewarm.
- Then blend

## Ingredients

- 1/2 lb hamberger
- 1/4 lb pork sausage (4-5 links)
- 1 grated carrot
- 1 grated potato
- 1 minced[?] onion
- heavy garlic, salt & pepper

## Method

- Saute all and cook well.
- Fill wonton or egg roll skins.
- Brown in hot oil, serve hot with cocktail sauce

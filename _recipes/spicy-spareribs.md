## Ingredients

- 1/2 cup sugar
- 1 cup vinegar
- 1 cup water
- 1/2 tsp chile powder
- 1/2 tsp allspice
- 1 lg. onion
- 3/4 cup catsup
- 2 Tbl. meat seasoning sauce
- 2 tsp salt
- dash of liquid smoke
- 3 lbs spareribs

## Method

- Cook in sauce for 30 minutes
- place in shallow pan and bake for 30 minutes in sauce
- or place on barbecue and cook 30 minutes basting with sauce the last 15 minutes.

## Ingredients

- 1/2 c butter
- 1/2 c sugar
- 2 c. cake flour
- pinch salt
- 1 c milk
- 1 t vanilla
- 3 squares (1 oz each) unsweetened baking choc.
- 2 egg whites
- 1/2 c sugar
- 3 t baking powder
- Frosting
    - 3/4 c butter
    - 3 egg yolks
    - 2 1/2 c powdered sugar
    - 6 oz semisweet choc. bits.
    - 2 T water

## Method

- Cream butter & sugar.
- Sift flour and resift with salt and powder.
- Add dry ingredients 1st then alternating with milk
- Beat until smooth after each addition
- Finely grate chocolate and add to batter.
- Beat egg whites till foamy and add sugar.
- Beat until stiff.
- Fold into batter
- Place into 3 8" greased and floured pans (or 2 9" pans)
- 350° for 20 min =25[?]
- Frosting: Beat 1st two then add sugar.
- 6 oz semisweet choc. bits. 2 T water.
- Melt in double boiler over water.
- Cool before putting on kake.

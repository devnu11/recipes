## Ingredients

- 1 lb hamberger - browned
- 5 strips bacon - diced cooked
- 2 chicken breasts - cubed cooked
- ~2 c. diced ham
- 1/2 head cabbage - shredded
- 3-4 green onion - sliced
- 3-4 celery stalked - diced
- 1 c. soy sauce
- 1/4 c. brown sugar
- 1 lb bean sprouts
- egg roll skins (2 pkgs)

## Method

- Cook[?] this mixture in 1 c. soy sauce and 1/4 c. brown sugar for 20 min.
- Add 1 lb bean sprouts.
- Dip egg roll skins (2 pkgs) in egg & water mixture.
- Fill and cook in oil
- may be frozen.

## Ingredients

- 8c. spinach
- 1c bean sprouts
- 1/2c. sliced water chestnuts
- 4 sliced bacon
- 1/4c. finely chopped onion
- 3T vinegar
- 3T cat sup
- 1T sugar

## Method

- Cook bacon, crumble and reserve fat.
- To it add onion vinegar, catsup, sugar
- heat through.
- Put on greens while still hot.

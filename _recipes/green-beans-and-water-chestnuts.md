## Ingredients

- 2 16 oz can french cut green beans
- 2 5 oz can water chestnuts
- 1 10 1/2 oz can condensed cream of mushroom soup
- 1/4 cup dry sherry wine
- 1/4 tsp crumbled dried oregano
- 1/2 tsp salt; dash pepper

## Method

- Drain beans and water chestnuts
- slice water chestnuts, thinly.
- Mix together soup, wine and spices
- toss with green beans and water chestnuts
- Place into greased baking dish and bake in 350 oven for about 25 minutes.

## Notes

Serves 8

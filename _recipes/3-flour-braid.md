## Ingredients

- [illegible] all-purpose flour
- [illegible] T sugar
- 1 T salt
- 2 pkg. yeast
- 1/4 c soft margarine
- 2 1/2 c warm water
- 1/4 c molasses
- 1 2/3 c whole wheat flour
- 1 t caraway seeds
- 2 T cocoa
- 1 1/2 c rye flour

## Method

- Mix 3 1/2 c flour with rest of ingredients in first column.
- Beat at medium speed 3 min.
- Divide batter into 3 even parts.
- Add 2 T molasses & whole wheat flour to one portion of batter.
- Mix in.
- Knead 5-6 min.
- Set aside to rise.
- To second portion, add 2 T molasses, caraway, rye, & cocoa.
- Repeat procedure.
- To third portion, add 1-1 1/4 c white flour & repeat procedure.
- Let rise until double (1 hr.)
- Punch down & halve each dough.
- Roll each piece into a rope 15" long.
- Make 2 braids.
- Let rise until double (45 min.)
- Bake at 350°F 35-40 min.

## Ingredients

- Tart Shells
    - 1 1/2 cups flour (sifted)
    - 2 Tbl. sugar
    - 6 Tbl. (3/4 stick) Margarine
    - 1 egg
    - 1 Tbl. cold water
- Strawberry Glaze -
    - 3 cups strawberries
    - 1/2 cup sugar
    - 2 Tbl. cornstarch
    - 1/8 tsp. salt
    - 1/2 cup water
- Cream Cheese Layer
    - 1 pkg. Cream Cheese (3-4 oz)
    - 2 Tbl. Confectioner Sugar
    - 1 Tbl. Cream.

## Method

- Bake at 425° for 10 minutes (6 tarts)
- Roll out half of pastry to 1/8" thickness.
- Cut out 3 6" rounds
- Fit into tart pans, pressing dough firmly against bottom,
- prick & bake in hot oven (425°) 10 minutes.
- Cool Completely on wire rack before removing from pan.
- Strawberry Glaze
- Save around 30 nice strawberries.
- Combine sugar, cornstarch, & salt in medium size saucepan
- slowly stir in water & remaining berries.
- Cook over low heat stirring constantly & mashing berries as they heat & thickens.
- Remove & cool slightly.
- Make cream cheese layer:
- Blend all ingredients in a small bowl until mixture is easy to spread.
- Spread mixture on bottom, top with berries (whole) & put glaze over all.
- Chill one hour or until set.

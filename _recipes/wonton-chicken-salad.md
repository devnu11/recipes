## Ingredients

- 1/4 cup cooking oil
- 1/4 cup vinegar
- 2 Tbl. sugar
- 1 TBL. sesame or cooking oil
- 10 wonton skins ( 3 inch squares)
- Cooking oil for shallow-fat-frying
- 6 cups desired torn greens
- 2 cups cubed, cooked chicken
- 1 11 oz can mandarin orange sections, drained
- 1 medium green or red pepper, seed and cut into 1/2 inch squares
- 4 green onions, bias-sliced into 1 inch lengths

## Method

For Dressing:

- In a screw-top jar combine the 1/4 cup oil , 1 tsp salt and the vinegar, sugar, cooking oil and 1/4 tsp pepper.
- Cover
- shake well, chill.
- Cut wonton skins into 1/4 inch-wide strips
- fry, a few at a time, in shallow hot fat about 1/2 to 1 minute or till crisp and golden, stirring occasionally.
- Drain on paper toweling.
- Line a salad bowl with desired torn greens.
- Over the greens arrange chicken, drained mandarin oranges, red or green pepper, green onions, and fried wonton strips.
- Shake dressing
- pour some over salad and toss.
- Pass remaining dressing

## Notes

Makes 6 servings.

Choose any combination of fresh greens and lettuce, such as, Bibb lettuce, bok choy, Boston lettuce; chinese cabbage, collard greens; dandelion greens endive, escarole, iceberg lettuce, kale, leaf lettuce mustard greens, parsley, red lead lettuce; romaine; spinach; turnip greens; watercress.

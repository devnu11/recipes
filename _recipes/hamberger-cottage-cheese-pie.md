## Method

- Sauté 1 lg onion and 1 lbs hamberger
- Season to taste
- Stir in 2 T. flour
- Line a 9" pie tin w/uncooked biscuit dough.
- Fill with hamberger mixture
- Fold 1 c. sm. curd cottage cheese into 2 eggs and beat slightly.
- Spread over hamberger & sprinkle w/paprika.
- Bake 30 min. at 375°.
- Serve in wedges w/tossed salad & vegies.

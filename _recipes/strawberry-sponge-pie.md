## Ingredients

- 1 c crushed strawberries
- 1 c sugar
- 1 env. gelatin
- 1/4 c cold water
- 1/4 t salt
- 1/2 c hot water
- 1 T lemon juice
- 1/2 c heavy cream (or evaporated milk)
- 2 egg whites
- 9" baked pie shell.

## Method

- Add sugar to berries and let stand 30 minutes.
- Soften gelatin in cold water then add salt and hot water.
- Stir until dissolved.
- Add berries & lemon juice.
- Cool
- When mixture begins to thicken fold in whipped cream and stiffly beaten egg whites.
- Chill in shell.
- Garnish with whole berries and whipped cream.

## Notes

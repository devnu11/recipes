## Ingredients

- 2/3 C oil
- 1/2 C white wine vinegar
- 1 T Dijon Mustard
- 1 t salt
- 1/2 t pepper
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 t. sweet basil leaves (1/2 t dried)
- 1 12 oz pkg Rotelle noodles
- 1 head cauliflower or broccoli flowerets, cooked
- 4 oz cooked green beans
- 1 can large ripe olives drained
- 1 C cherry tomato halves
- 1 C Jack cheese cubed (1/4 lb)

## Method

- Combine oil, vinegar, mustard, salt pepper, garlic & basil.
- Cook pasta, drain & rinse in cold water.
- Toss dressing with pasta and remaining ingredients.

## Notes

Sunset

makes 12 cups salad.

## Ingredients

- 6 cups sifted flour
- 1 cup butter or oleo
- 3 eggs
- 1/2 - 8c yeast (household large cake yeast)
- 1tsp salt
- 4 tbl sugar
- 1/2 pint of sour cream
- 1/2 cup warm milk

FILLING

- 2 lbs walnuts ground
- 1 1/2 cups white sugar
- 1 tbl Brown sugar
- 4 egg yolks slight beaten
- 4 egg whites

## Method

- Dissolve yeast in warm milk.
- Combine flour butter mix like for pie.
- Add salt, sugar, beaten eggs, sour cream add yeast mixture blend well.
- Divide dough into six parts
- Spread with nut filling - place rolls to until double in bulk
- Bake 35 to 40 minutes

FILLING

- Mix above well, then fold in 4 egg whites.

## Notes

Handwritten notes: 1lb = 2c 1cup; no longer than 13"; (egg whites) beaten until stiff but not at all dry.

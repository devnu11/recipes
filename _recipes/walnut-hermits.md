## Ingredients

- 1 cup shortening
- 2 cups brown sugar
- 2 eggs
- 3 1/2 cups sifted enriched flour
- 1 tsp baking powder
- 1 tsp soda
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 2 tsp cinnamon
- 1 tsp nutmeg
- 1/2 cup sour milk or buttermilk *
- 1 cup broken Calif. walnuts
- 2 cups seeded raisins
- 1 cup dates, cut up

## Method

- Thoroughly cream shortening and sugar; add eggs and beat well.
- Sift dry ingredients together; add to creamed misture alternately with sour milk.
- Add nuts and fruits; drop by heaping tsps onto greased cooky sheet.
- Press walnut half lightly into top of each cooky, if desired.
- Bake in oven (375) 10 to 12 minutes.

## Notes

Old fashioned hermits, loaded with walnuts, raisins and dates.

Makes about 6 dozen.

*(add 2 tsp vinegar to 1/2 cup milk)

## Ingredients

- 1 pkj frozen hash browns - put in a 9X13" pan
- 1/4 C (or more) margarine - melt
- 1/3 C chopped onion
    - toss into potatoes & pour margarine over
- 1/2 C. grated cheese
    - (bracketed with onion) toss into potatoes & pour margarine over
- 1 Can cr. of chicken soup (or mushroom)
    - blend & pour over potatoes
- 1 pint Sour cream
    - (bracketed with soup) blend & pour over potatoes
- 3 Tb. (or more margarine - melt
    - blend - top casserole
- 3/4 C. corn flake crumbs
    - (bracketed with margarine) blend - top casserole

## Method

- Bake 350° 30 - 45 minutes - 'til bubbly

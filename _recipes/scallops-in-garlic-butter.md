## Ingredients

- 2 cloves garlic
- 1/2 lb butter
- parsley
- 1/2 t tarragon
- salt & pepper
- Scallops
- 1/2 T sallions[?]

## Method

- Brown garlic in butter.
- Add seasonings
- pour over scallops and Broil for 3 min on each side.

## Ingredients

- 1 C. unsalted Butter, nearly melted
- 1 C. Sugar
- 1/2 tsp. Salt
- 1 Box Alpine Apple Cidermix
- 2 eggs
- 1 Tsp vanilla
- 1 tsp b. Soda
- 1/2 tsp b. powder
- 3 Cups Flour
- 1 Bag Caramels

## Method

- Pre heat 350°
- Parchment
- Cream Butter, Sugar, Salt + All[?] cider till Smooth + fluffy
- add eggs + vanilla
- B. Soda + powder Flour

## Notes

- ~~12-14 mins.~~ 15-20 min
- Use 2oz Scoop
- 2 1/2 dozen

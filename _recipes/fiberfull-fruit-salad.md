## Ingredients

- Dressing
    - 6T oj
    - 3T oil
    - 2t honey
    - 1t grated orange peel
    - 1/2t salt
    - 1/2t pepper
- Salad
    - 1 lg. red apple cored & diced
    - 1 lg. pear cored & diced
    - 6 cups spinach coarsely chopped
    - 1 bunch watercress
    - 1/2 sm. red onion thinly sliced
    - 1 pt strawberries or 1 1/2 cup cranberries chopped
    - 3T golden raisins
    - 2 medium oranges peeled and sectioned
    - 2T sliced natural almonds - toasted
    - 2T pumpkin seeds.

## Method

- (Dressing) combine; makes 1/2 cup
- Combine apple & pear with 1/4 cup dressing cool 2 hours
- Combine cranberries, raisins and 2T sugar set for 2 hours
- Toss spinach, watercress, onion & remaining d[illegible]
- Arrange on lg. platter with apple & pear mix in[?] center; cranberries, raisins & oranges Surrounding

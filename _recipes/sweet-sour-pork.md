## Ingredients

- 1 1/2 lb. lean pork shoulder cut in strips
- 1 #2 can (2 1/2 cups) pineapple chunks
- 1/4 cup brown sugar
- 2 Tbl. cornstarch
- 1/4 cup vinegar
- 2 to 3 Tbl. soy sauce
- 1/2 tsp. salt
- 1 small green pepper, cut in strips
- 1/4 cup thinly sliced onion

## Method

- Brown pork in small amount of fat.
- Add 1/2 cup water
- cover and simmer (do not boil) till tender about 1 hr.
- Drain pineapple, reserving syrup.
- Combine sugar & cornstarch
- add pineapple syrup, vinegar, soy sauce & salt.
- Add to pork
- Cook & stir till gravy thickens.
- Add pineapple, green pepper, and onion.
- Cook 2 or 3 minutes
- serve over rice

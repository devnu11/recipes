## Method

- Brown six lean loin chops, put 1 cup raw rice in bottom of large casserole, place browned chops on top of rice
- pour one can of consomme plus 1/2 can water over all.
- Slice a large raw onion into 6 slices, put one on top of each chop.
- Do the same with a tomato and green pepper.
- Add salt and pepper to taste plus a pinch of thyme and marjoram.
- Cover and bake at 350 deg. for one hour

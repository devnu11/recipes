## Ingredients

- 1/2 c oil
- 2T vinegar
- 2T lemon juice
- 2T catsup
- 3/4t salt
- 1/2t dry mustard
- 1/4t paprika
- 1t sugar

## Method

- mix all well.
- Can also use pinch of garlic, hint of dill or basil
- good on avacado and mandarin orange salad

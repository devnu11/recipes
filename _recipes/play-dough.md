## Ingredients

- 1 Cup Flour
- 1 Cup Water
- 1 Tbs. Oil
- 1/2 Cup Salt
- 1 Tsp. Cream of Tartar
- Food Coloring

## Method

- Cook over medium heat until mixture pulls away from sides of pan and becomes like playdough in consistency.
- Knead until cool.

## Notes

Keeps 3 months unrefrigerated.

## Ingredients

- 1 lb Ground beef
- 14 oz can red kidney beans
    - niblets corn
    - stewed tomatoes
- 8 oz tomato sauce
- 1/2 bottle taco sauce (4 oz or to taste) Not Salsa
- 1 t chili powder
- 1/2 t cumin
- onion, diced.

## Method

- Brown meat and onion.
- Drain fat.
- Add cans with liquid.
- Add Seasoning.
- Heat.
- Serve with shredded cheese and sour cream and chopped avacado. chips.

## Notes

Serves 6

"Not Salsa" is underlined in the original.

"niblets corn" and "stewed tomatoes" are written indented under "14 oz can red kidney beans", apparently sharing the can size.

An illegible mark (possibly a quantity) precedes "onion, diced."

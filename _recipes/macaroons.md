## Ingredients

- 2/3 cup (1/2 15 oz can) sweetened condensed milk
- 3 cups shredded coconut (two 4 oz packages)
- 1 tsp vanilla
- 2/3 tsp almond extract

## Method

- Mix together sweetened condensed milk, coconut and flavorings.
- Drop by tablespoonfuls about 1 inch apart on a well greased cookie sheet.
- Bake in moderate oven (350) about 8 to 10 minutes or until delicately browned around edges.
- Remove from baking sheet immediately.

## Notes

Yield: 2 1/2 dozen

For chocolate chip macaroons: Substitute 1 package (6z) semi sweet chocolate pieces for 1 cup coconut, Bake 10 to 12 minutes.

For nut macaroons: Substitute 1 cup coarsely chopped nut meats for 1 cup of the coconut and bake 10 to 12 minutes

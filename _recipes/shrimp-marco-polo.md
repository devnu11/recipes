## Ingredients

- 1/2 cup chopped onion
- 1/2 cup chopped celery
- 2 Tbl. butter or margarine
- 1 cup fresh sliced mushrooms
- 1 clove garlic, minced
- 1 1 lb can whole tomatoes, chopped
- 1 8 oz can tomato sauce
- 1 tsp. seasoned salt
- 1/8 tsp. tabasco sauce
- 2 Tbl. parsley flakes
- 1 lb fresh bean sprouts
- 1 1/4 cups cooked or canned shrimp

## Method

- Saute onion and celery in butter until limp;
- add mushrooms and saute 1 minute.
- Stir in garlic tomatoes, tomato sauce, salt, tabasco and parsley flakes.
- Simmer covered, 25 min. to blend flavors;
- stir occasionally.
- Meanwhile wash bean sprouts and place in saucepan leaving on excess water.
- Sprinkle with salt.
- Cover and simmer 10 minutes.
- toss to cook evenly just until crisp tender.
- Add shrimp to sauce, stir to mis.
- Let stand a few minutes to heat shrimp.
- DO NOT BOIL.
- Serve over hot bean sprouts.

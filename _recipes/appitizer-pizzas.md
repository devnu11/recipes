## Ingredients

- 2 cans biscuts cooked, halved & centers removed
- 1 lb cheder cheese
- 2 cans chopped olives
- small grated onion
- 1 T vinegar
- 1 T oil
- 1 can pizza sauce

## Method

- mix above & spoon into biscut halves.
- broil or bake on high til cheese bubbly.

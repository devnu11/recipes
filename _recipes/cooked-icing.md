## Ingredients

- 3 T flour
- 1 c milk
- 1 c butter
- 1 c sugar
- 1 t vanilla

## Method

- Blend flour and milk.
- Cook over low heat till smooth and thickened.
- Cool
- Cream butter and sugar.
- Add cooked mixture and vanilla.

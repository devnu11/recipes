## Ingredients

- 1 9" graham cracker crust
- 2 bananas
- 1 8 oz cream cheese
- 2 cups milk
    - 1/2 cup added gradually to softened cream cheese
    - remaining milk added with pudding mix
- 1 3 1/4 oz Instant Vanilla Pudding
- Toasted Coconut

## Method

- Slice bananas onto graham cracker crust.
- Gradually add 1/2 cup milk to softened cream cheese mixing until well blended.
- Add pudding mix & remaining milk
- beat slowly 1 minute.
- Pour into crust
- chill
- garnish with coconut -

## Ingredients

- 3 Tbsp yeast
- 3/4 Cu warm water
- drizzle molasses - Set aside to grow
- 1 T salt
- 2 Cu boiling water
- 1/2 Cu shortening
- 1/2 Cu molasses
- 2 Cu whole[?] wheat flour
- 2 Cu Oats
- 1 Cu 7 grain cereal
- 4 1/2-5 Cu White flour

## Method

- mix Thoroughly - add yeast mixture
- Knead 15-20 mins, rise, punch,

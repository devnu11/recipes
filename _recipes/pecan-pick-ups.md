## Ingredients

- Pastry:
    - 3 oz pkg cream cheese
    - 1 stick butter
    - 1 cup flour
- Filling:
    - 3/4 cup brown sugar
    - 1 Tbl. milk
    - 1 Tbl. melted butter
    - 1/2 cup chopped pecans
    - 1 tsp. vanilla
    - 1 egg

## Method

- Bake 350° about 20 minutes
- Mix - chill divide into 24 balls.
- Put in muffin tins press evenly around sides & bottom. (small size muffin tins)
- Mix and put filling into each Muffin tin.

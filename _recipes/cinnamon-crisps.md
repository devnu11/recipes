## Ingredients

- 1/2 cup butter or margarine
- 1 cup brown sugar
- 1 egg
- 1 Tbl shredded orange peel
- 1 1/2 cups sifted enriched flour
- 1 tsp. baking powder
- 1 tsp. cinnamon
- 1/4 tsp. salt
- 1/2 cup bran flakes
- 1/4 cup chopped pecans

## Method

- Thoroughly cream butter and sugar.
- Beat in egg and orange peel.
- Sift together dry ingredients;
- add to creamed mixture.
- Stir in bran flakes and pecans.
- Chill 1 hour.
- Shape in 2 long 1 1/2 inch rolls.
- Wrap in waxed paper;
- chill well.
- Slice 1/8 to 1/4 inch thick.
- Bake on greased cookie sheet in moderate oven (350) 8 to 10 minutes.

## Notes

Makes 4 doz.

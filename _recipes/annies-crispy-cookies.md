## Ingredients

- 1 cup shortening
- 1 cup brown sugar
- 1 cup granulated sugar
- 2 eggs, beaten slightly
- 1 tsp vanilla
- 2 cups corn flakes
- 2 cups shredded coconut
- 2 cups flour
- 1 tsp. each baking soda & baking powder
- 1/2 tsp. salt

## Method

- Cream shortening with the sugars.
- Mix in eggs & vanilla
- stir in corn flakes and coconut.
- Sift & measure flour; sift again with baking soda, baking powder and salt.
- Blend into other mixture.
- Roll dough into walnut-sized balls; place them on a greased cooky sheet about 4" apart.
- Flatten the balls with a fork
- Bake in a mod. hot oven (375°) for about 12 min, or until golden brown.
- Remove immediately from the cooky sheet to cool.

## Notes

Makes about 60 cookies

## Ingredients

- 1 (14-ounce) can Eagle® Brand Sweetened Condensed Milk (NOT evaporated milk)
- 1 1/2 cups cold water
- 1 (4-serving size) package instant vanilla flavor pudding and pie filling mix
- 2 cups (1 pint) whipping cream, whipped
- 36 vanilla wafers
- 3 medium bananas, sliced and dipped in lemon juice

## Method

- In large bowl, combine sweetened condensed milk and water.
- Add pudding mix
- beat well.
- Chill 5 minutes.
- Fold in whipped cream.
- Spoon 1 cup pudding mixture into 2 1/2-quart glass serving bowl.
- Top with one-third each of the wafers, bananas and pudding.
- Repeat layering twice, ending with pudding.
- Chill thoroughly.
- Garnish as desired.
- Refrigerate leftovers.

## Notes

Makes 8 to 10 servings

## Ingredients

- 1 c. water
- 1 stick margarine
- 3 T cocoa
- 1/2 c. shortening
- 2 c. flour
- 2 c. sugar
- 1/2 t salt
- 2 eggs
- 1 t vanilla
- 1/2 c buttermilk
- 1 t soda
- Frosting
    - 1 stick margarine
    - 3 T cocoa
    - 6 T canned milk
    - 1 box powdered sugar
    - 1 t vanilla
    - 3/4 c chopped nuts.

## Method

- Bring to a boil
- pour hot mixture into: 2 c. flour, 2 c. sugar, 1/2 t salt
- beat; then add: 2 eggs, 1 t vanilla, 1/2 c buttermilk, 1 t soda
- put in greased: flour cookie sheet
- Bake 350° for 20-25 min.
- Frosting: melt these and add: 1 box powdered sugar, 1 t vanilla, 3/4 c chopped nuts.
- Spread as soon as cake comes from oven.

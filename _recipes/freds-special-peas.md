## Ingredients

- frozen peas
- butter, salt and pepper
- 1 Tbl. mayonnaise
- 1 Tbl sour cream
- 1 Tsp prepared horseradish

## Method

- Cook frozen peas
- drain season with butter, salt and pepper/
- Then add 1 Tbl.mayonnaise, 1 Tbl sour cream, 1 Tsp prepared horseradish.
- Mix well into peas.

## Notes

Can use green beans or spinach.

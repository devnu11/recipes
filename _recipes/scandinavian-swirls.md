## Ingredients

- 1 cup blanched almonds, ground
- 1 1/3 cup confectioners sugar
- 1 egg white
- 1/4 tsp almond extract
- Candied cherries, orange peel or nuts

## Method

- Mix nuts and sugar in medium sized bowl.
- Beat egg white slightly with fork in small bowl;
- add gradually to sugar mixture;
- blend well.
- Add almond extract.
- Put through pastry tube or drop from tip of teaspoon onto well greased and floured cooky sheets.
- Decorate with candied cherries, orange peel or nuts.
- Bake in moderate oven (350) 15 minutes or until golden around edges
- Cool on wire cake racks.

## Notes

Makes 15 macaroons

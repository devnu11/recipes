## Ingredients

- 1 large head of lettuce, shredded
- 6 green onions, chopped
- 1/4 cup slivered almonds
- 1/2 cup sesame seeds
- 1 pkg chicken flavored Top Ramen
- 1/4 cup butter
- Dressing
    - 1/2 cup salad oil
    - 1[?] tsp sesame oil
    - 1 Tbs. soy sauce
    - 1/3 cup sugar
    - 1/4 cup vinegar
    - 1/4 tsp. salt
    - 1/4 tsp. pepper

## Method

- Smash Top Ramen in package before opening
- Brown Ramen in melted Butter with sesame seeds & seasoning packet.
- Mix with remaining ingredients
- Dressing on back →
- (Dressing) Mix & pour over salad & toss before serving

## Ingredients

- 4 veal cutlets 1/4" thick (1 lb)
- 1/2 c plain dry bread crumbs
- 2 1/4 ts[?] grated parmesan cheese
- 1 egg
- 1 T water
- 1 T olive oil
- 1 1/2 c. spagetti sauce
- 1/2 c. shredded mozerella cheese (2oz)
- 1/4 t oregeno

## Method

- Pound veal to 1/8" thickness.
- Combine bread and Cheese
- Beat egg w/ water.
- Dip meat in egg mixture and then in bread mixture.
- Cook w/ olive oil in skillet.
- Arrange in shallow 2qt baking dish.
- Pour sauce over meat
- Sprinkle with cheese and oregano.
- Bake 375° 20-30 min

## Notes

Heart Smart

serves 4.

## Ingredients

- 2 lb. small yellow squash or zucchini
- 1 Tbl. butter or margarine
- 1/2 cup chopped green onion
- 1 tsp. salt
- dash pepper
- 1 can (1 lb) stewed tomatoes

## Method

- Wash squash or zucchini.
- Cut them crosswise, on the diagonal, into slices 1/2 inch thick.
- in Hot butter in a large skillet, saute chopped green onion until it is tender - about 3 min.
- Add the slicked squash, salt and pepper.
- toss together lightly.
- Cook covered over low heat, about 15 minutes or just until squash is tender.
- Add the stewed tomatoes, and toss to combine.
- Cook, covered 1 minute longer, or until mixture is heated through.

## Notes

Makes 8 servings, 44 calories each.

## Ingredients

- 1 egg
- 1/2 cup lowfat milk
- 1 t salt
- 1/2 t garlic salt
- 1/4 cup flour
- 3/4 cup bisquick
- 1 1/2 t baking powder
- 1/4 cup red onion, finely chopped
- 2 pkgs 9[?] oz thawed, drained artichoke hearts
- 1 T parsley

## Method

- Beat egg and add dry ingredients.
- Add onion and parsley by hand.
- Batter should be thickness of heavy cream.
- fry at 350-375° in electric fry pan till golden brown.

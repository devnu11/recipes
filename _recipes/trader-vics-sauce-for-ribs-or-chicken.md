## Ingredients

- 1 inch ginger root - chopped or sheid
- 1 clove garlic - sheid
- 1/2 cup soy sauce
- 1/2 cup catsup
- 3/4 cup sugar
- 2[?] Tbl. sherry or whiskey
- 1 Tbl salt
- Optional: Worchester sauce or peppercorn

## Method

- Marinate 2-2 1/2 lbs ribs overnite if possible - or all day.
- remove from sauce and bake in 350 deg. 1 to 1 1/2 hours.
- You can spoon a bit of sauce over if you like while baking.

## Notes

Also excellent with chicken.

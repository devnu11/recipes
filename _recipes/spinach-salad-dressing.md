## Ingredients

- 2/3 c oil
- 1/4 c wine vinegar
- 1/4-1T curry powder
- 1T dry mustard
- 2T soy sauce
- 1/2 lb Bacon
- 2 eggs hard boiled
- 2 bunch Spinach salad

## Method

- mix together & enjoy

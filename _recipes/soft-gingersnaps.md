## Ingredients

- 4 c. flour
- 4 t soda
- 1 t salt
- 2 T ginger
- 2 t. cinnamon
- 1 1/2 c oil
- 2 c sugar
- 2 eggs
- 1/2 c molasses

## Method

- Sift first 5 ingredients.
- Cream oil, sugar eggs & molasses.
- Add dry ingredients by hand.
- Make into medium-sized balls, roll in 1/3 c. sugar. (Don't Flatten)
- Bake at 350° on ungreased sheet about 8 min.
- Do Not OVERBAKE!

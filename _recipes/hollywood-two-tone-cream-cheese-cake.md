## Ingredients

- Crust for Hollywood Two-Tone Cream Cheese Cake
    - 1 1/4 cups graham cracker or zwieback crumbs (crush until very fine with rolling pin or put through food mill. Sift and measure). Very light bread crumbs combined with 1/4 teaspoon cinnamon may be used
    - 1/4 cup granulated sugar
    - 1/4 cup melted butter or margarine
- First Part of Filling
    - 1 pound cream cheese (comes in 1/2-pound packages)
    - 1/2 cup granulated sugar
    - 3 medium eggs or 2 very large (1/2 cupful)
    - 3/4 teaspoon vanilla
- Second Part of Filling
    - pint commercial sour cream (do not use home-type sour cream)
    - 1/4 cup granulated sugar
    - 1 teaspoon vanilla

## Method

- Combine all ingredients and mix thoroughly, using fingers or fork until well blended and moist.
- Grease a 9- or 10-inch loose-bottom round pan (without tube) with 1 teaspoon unsalted shortening.
- Cover sides of pan with about 1 cup of crumb mixture, having it come up to about 1 inch from top of pan.
- Pour rest of crumb mixture into bottom of pan, and with a large spoon press down until smooth and compact.
- Keep in refrigerator or cool place while preparing filling.
- First Part of Filling: Combine all ingredients, and with electric mixer or heavy wooden spoon beat until smooth, or for about 2 or 3 minutes.
- Pour into pan, spread until smooth and uniform.
- Bake in preheated oven, at 375° F., for 20 minutes.
- Remove from oven, place on cake rack, and let stand in room temperature for about 15 minutes.
- Raise temperature of oven to 475° F.
- About a minute or two before time is up, prepare second part of filling.
- Second Part of Filling: With a spoon mix these ingredients only until well blended.
- Pour over cool baked first part of filling, starting from side to center by tablespoonful.
- Spread evenly, as gently as possible.
- Bake in 475° F. oven for about 10 minutes.
- Remove from oven and let stand in room temperature on cake rack for 5 or 6 hours, or until absolutely cold before removing cake from pan.
- When filling is cold, go around sides of cake with a spatula or dinner knife
- release clamp and remove cake carefully.
- Do not cut until following day.
- Cover cake with wax paper and store in coldest part of refrigerator for at least 12 hours or longer.
- This cake may be kept for 3 or 4 days if covered with wax paper and refrigerated.
- When ready to serve, cut into wedges and with a spatula or server release carefully from bottom of pan.
- For special occasions, decorate top of each with pink cream-cheese rosettes and green leaves.

## Notes

Special remarks. Remove all ingredients for this cake from refrigerator an hour before preparation.

Entire top of cake may be covered with extra crumb mixture before baking, but cake is more attractive and unusual-looking with plain top. Before releasing cake from pan, loosen crumbs on sides of pan, allowing them to drop on edge of cake. This forms a rim, which imparts a more finished appearance.

Eggs in first part of filling may be separated. Beat yolks with other ingredients. Then beat whites until they cling to bowl; fold into cheese mixture until well blended and no streaks appear. This method will give a slightly higher cake with a fluffier texture, but not as smooth as with first method. We prefer first method.

This cake is about 1 1/4 inches high when ready to be served.

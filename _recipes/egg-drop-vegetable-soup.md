## Ingredients

- 2 32 oz cans (8 cups) chicken broth
- 1 Tbl. molasses
- 2 beaten eggs
- 2 Tbl. water
- 1 10 oz package frozen Chinese style vegetables
- 1 6 oz pkg frozen tiny shrimp
- 1/2 of a 7 oz. pkg (42) 4 inch square wonton skins, cut into 1 inch strips and separated
- 5 green onions, sliced.

## Method

- In Dutch oven or kettle combine chicken broth and molasses
- bring to boiling.
- In small bowl combine eggs and water
- set aside.
- To boiling broth add frozen vegetables, frozen shrimp, wonton strips, and green onions
- return to boiling. stirring to separate vegetables.
- Slowly pour in beaten egg mixture, stirring constantly.
- Reduce heat and simmer, uncovered, 5 minutes.

## Notes

Makes 10 servings.

This is excellent Jacky!

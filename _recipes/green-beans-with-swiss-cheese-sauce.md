## Ingredients

- 1 Tbl butter
- 1 Tbl flour
- 1/2 tsp each sugar and salt
- 1/8 tsp pepper
- 1/4 cup milk
- 1/2 tsp grated onion
- 1/2 cup sour cream
- 2 cans small whole green beans, drained
- 1 1/2 cups shredded swiss cheese
- 1/3 cup corn flake crumbs
- 1 T melted butter

## Method

- In a small pan, melt butter and thoroughly blend in flour, salt and sugar and pepper
- cook stirring until bubbly.
- Blend in milk, remove from the heat, and stir in the onion and sour cream until well mixed.
- Combine sauce with green beans and cheese, and turn into a buttered 1 1/2 qt. casserole.
- Combine cornflake crumbs with melted butter
- spread on top[?]
- Bake in a hot oven (400) for about 20 minutes.

## Notes

good with Roast beef or lamb roast

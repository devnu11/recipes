## Ingredients

- 1 1/2 pounds Jonathan or Winesap apples peeled, cored, and cut into about 16 sections
- 2 tablespoons lemon juice
- 1/2 cup granulated sugar
- 1/4 teaspoon salt
- 1/2 cup hot water
- About 1 tablespoon cornstarch
- 2 tablespoons cold water
- 1 tablespoon scalded raisins or currants may be added (will darken mixture a little)
- 2 tablespoons coarsely broken pecans or walnuts

## Method

- Combine hot water, granulated sugar, and salt.
- Allow to come to boiling point, and when clear add apple slices over which lemon juice has been poured.
- Cover and cook very slowly (about 15 minutes) until slices are tender but not too soft.
- Pick up with perforated ladle and drain thoroughly.
- Combine the cornstarch and cold water and add to the hot sirup.
- Cook until thick, and then add to apples, mixing gently until they take on a glaze.
- Allow to stand until cold
- then fold into apple mixture the scalded raisins or currants and the nuts.

## Notes

This filling belongs to an "Apple Squares" recipe whose beginning is on an earlier printed cookbook page (page 454, "DESSERTS AND CHEESE CAKES") that is not in the scans. The dough/assembly text at the top of page 115, verbatim:

if necessary. All lard or vegetable shortening may be used. Use a square pan 7 x 7 x 1 inches. Use 2/3 of dough for bottom crust and the rest for upper crust. (For a square pan 9 x 9 x 1 1/2 inches, double recipe of filling and make 1 1/2 recipes of dough.) Make filling (below) and pour entire mixture into crust that has been sprinkled with bread crumbs. Dot top of apples with 1 tablespoon of butter and sprinkle with a little nutmeg and cinnamon. Brush ends of dough with milk, place upper crust on, and seal well with fingers or fork, so that crusts do not open in baking. Brush top with milk, prick with fork, and bake at 425° F. for about 40 minutes, or until brown. Allow to cool on cake rack. Remove cake from pan when cool, and cover with icing made with confectioners' sugar, vanilla, and 1 tablespoon of hot milk. Use more sugar or milk if necessary. Let stand until icing is absolutely set and cake is cool. Then, with a saw-edge knife, cut carefully into squares or rectangles. These squares may be served in paper cups and tops may be decorated with tiny buttercream or cream-cheese rosettes and green leaves. Serves 8 to 12.

## Ingredients

- 1/2 cup vegetable shortening
- 1/4 cup butter or margarine
- 2 cups powdered sugar
- 1 tsp. vanilla
- 2 Tbl. milk ← use buttermilk especially on banana cake

## Method

- Cream shortening add sugar and milk and beat until creamy then add vanilla.
- Spread

## Notes

Jacky: this will cover the loaf cake very generously, but you will have to double the recipte for a 2 layer cake.

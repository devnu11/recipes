## Ingredients

- 1 tbl flour
- 1/2 teasp. salt
- 1 lb sirloin
- 2 tbl butter
- 1 cup thinly sliced mushrooms
- 1/2 cup chopped onion
- 1 clove garlic, minced
- 2 tbl butter
- 3 tbl flour
- 1 tbl tomoto paste
- 1 can condensed beef broth
- 1 cup sour cream
- 1 tbl. sherry

## Method

- Combine Tbl flour & the salt, dredge meat thoroughly.
- Heat skillet then add 2 Tbl. butter melted, add the sirloin strips & brown flipping meat by turning all sides.
- Add mushroom slices, onion, & garlic, cook 3 or 4 minutes or intil onion is barely tender.
- Time to make rich sauce easiest if you first remove the meat & mushrooms from skillet.
- add 2 Tbl. butter to pandrippings;
- when melted blend in 3 tbl flour.
- Add the tomato paste.
- Now slowly pour in cold meat stock - cook stirring constantly until mixure thickens.
- Return browned meat & mushrooms to skillet.
- Stir in sour cream & sherry;
- heat briefly.
- Serve with noodles or pilaf or rice.

## Ingredients

- 1 C sugar (+ 1/2)
- 4 T cornstarch
- 1/2 C water
- 1 t lemon juice.
- 2 C whole berries
- 1 pie shell

## Method

- Add all but whole berries.
- Cook till thick, Stir always, and cool slightly.
- Pour over whole berries, chill

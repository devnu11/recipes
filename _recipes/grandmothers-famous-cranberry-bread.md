## Ingredients

- 2 c sifted flour
- 1 c sugar
- 1 1/2 t. baking powder
- 1 t salt
- 1/2 t baking soda
- 1/4 c. butter or margarine
- 1 egg, beaten
- 1 t grated orange peel
- 3/4 c. oj
- 1 1/2 c light raisins
- 1 1/2 c fresh or froz. cranberries, chopped.

## Method

- Sift flour, sugar baking powder, soda, salt in lg. bowl.
- Cut in butter until crumbly.
- Add egg, orange peel, oj all at once.
- Stir just until mixture is evenly moist.
- Fold in raisins & cranberries
- Spoon into greased 9x5x3" loaf pan.
- Bake 350 for 1 hr. 10 min or until toothpick inserted into center comes out clean.
- Remove from pan
- cool on wire rack.

## Notes

May substitute cranberries for raisins to have all cranberry bread.

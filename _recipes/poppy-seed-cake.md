## Ingredients

- 1 pkg Duncan Hines yellow cake mix
- 4 eggs
- 8 oz. sour cream
- 3/4 c. salad oil
- 1/4 c. poppy seeds

## Method

- Grease and flour pan - use tube pan.
- Mix all ingredients thoroughly.
- Bake 325 for 45 min.
- Cool 15 min. and remove.

## Ingredients

- 9" pie shell - baked
- 6-8 bacon slices cooked and crumbed
- 1 1/2 c sliced mushrooms } saute in butter
- 1 sm onion, chopped }
- 1/2 (10 oz) pkg froz spinach thawed & draind
- 4 eggs
- 2 c heavy cream
- 1/4 t salt
- pepper
- 1/2 c monterey jack cheese
- 1/2 c mild cheader
- 1/2 c mozarella
- 1 c diced ham

## Method

- Add spinach to mushroom mix.
- Beat eggs, cream, salt & pepper
- mix in cheeses
- put 1/2 bacon and all the ham on bottom.
- add spinach mix and pour egg mix on top.
- Top with rest of bacon
- Bake 350° 30-45 min til set

## Notes

Mushrooms and onion are joined by a brace marked "saute in butter" (a word before "in butter" is crossed out).

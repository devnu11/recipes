## Method

First kind:

- Measure into a bowl two cups of sugar, one cup soft butter and two eggs;
- mix very well, using a wooden spoon.
- Add one cup raisins.
- Measure and sift together 2 1/4 cups flour, one tsp baking soda and one-half tsp. salt.
- Add to shortening mixture, then stir in two cups quick cooking oats.
- Mix well but gently.
- Drop by teaspoonfuls onto cooky sheet;
- flatten and bake in 400 degree oven for 10 minutes or until crisp and brown.
- Cookies should not be placed too close together on baking sheet, as they spread into rather large cookies.

Second kind:

- Mix together one cup shortening, one cup brown sugar, one cup granulated sugar, two beaten eggs, one tsp vanilla, 1 1/2 cups flour, one tsp. soda, one tsp. salt, three cups quick cooking oats and one half cup chopped nuts.
- Shape into a long roll or press into waxed paper lined ice cube trays.
- Wrap well in waxed paper.
- Chill in refrigerator.
- Slice one-fourth inch thick.
- Bake on ungreased cooky sheet in 350 oven for 10 minutes.

Third kind:

- Cream one-half cup shortening, one-half cup granulated sugar and one half cup brown sugar.
- Add one well beaten egg.
- Stir together one cup all purpose flour, one half tsp salt, one half tsp baking powder and one-half tsp soda.
- Add to first mixture.
- Blend in one-half cup coconut, one cup oatmeal and one tsp vanilla.
- Drop by teaspoonfuls onto ungreased cooky sheet,
- Bake in 350 oven for 15 minutes.

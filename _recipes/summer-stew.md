## Ingredients

- 2 # boneless beef chuck, cut 1" piece[s?]
- 1/4 cup flour
- 1/2 tsp. salt
- 1/4 tsp. each pepper and thyme
- 1/8 tsp. ground cloves
- 1/4 cup butter, margarine or bacon drippings
- 3 lg. onions thinly sliced
- 1 can beef broth
- 1/3 cup dry red wine or water
- 1 Tbl. catsup

## Method

- Coat the meat all over in flour mixed with the salt, pepper, thyme and cloves, saving any extra flour mixture.
- Using a dutch oven or other heavy pan saute the meat in the butter until it is browned on all sides
- Push the meat to one side of the pan and add onions
- saute about 5 min. or until onions are limp and brown.
- Sprinkle reserved flour mixture over meat and onions, stirring and scraping bottom of pan.
- Stir in beef broth, wine or water, and catsup.
- Cover the pan, reduce heat, and simmer slowly for about 1 1/2 hours or until the meat is fork tender.
- serve immediately or reheat and serve.

## Notes

a rich, flavorful sauce distinguishes this meat dish; serve it over mashed[?] potatoes, rice, or spaghetti. The stew improves with reheating.

Makes about 6 servings

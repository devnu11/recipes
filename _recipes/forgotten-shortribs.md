## Ingredients

- About 1 1/2 tsp. salt
- About 1/2 tsp pepper
- 3 to 4 lbs beef shortribs
- 1 can (8 oz) tomato sauce
- 2 Tbl molasses
- 2 Tbl cider vinegar
- 1 tsp liquid smoke flavoring
- 1 Tbl instant minced onion

## Method

- Sprinkle salt and pepper on all sides of the shortribs and put into a Dutch oven or 3 qt. casserole.
- In a small pan combine the tomato sauce with the molasses, vinegar, liquid smoke, and instant minced onion
- bring to a boil and simmer for 5 minutes.
- Pour over the shortribs
- cover and bake in a slow oven(275) for 3 to 4 hours, or until tender.
- Just before serving, spoon off any excess fat.
- (If you prepare this dish a day ahead, refrigerate it, and then lift of[f?] the solidified fat before you reheat it).

## Notes

These shortribs accumulate a delicious, slow-baked flavor as they cook.

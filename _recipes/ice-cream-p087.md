## Ingredients

- 4 cups fruit
- 1 1/2 cups sugar
- 1 c evaporated milk
- 1 t vanilla
- 1/4 c lemon juice
- 1 egg
- 1 sm carton whipping cream
- milk to fill ice cream maker to line

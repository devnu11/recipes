## Ingredients

- 1 cup butter
- 2 cups sifted flour
- 1 1/4 cups sour cream (thick or commercial)
- 1 cup apricot preserves
- 1 Tbl. lemon juice

NUT FILLING:

- 1 1/2 cups nuts, chopped (almonds, pecans or walnuts)
- 1 cup chopped coconut
- 1/2 cup chopped raisins
- 1/4 cup chopped maraschino cherries if desired

## Method

- Bake at 350 for 30 to 40 minutes
- Cut: 1 cup butter into 2 cups sifted flour until particles are the size of peas
- Add: 1 1/4 cups sour cream (thick or commercial)
- stir until dough is moist enough to hold together.
- Wrap dough in foil;
- chill 5 to 6 hours or overnight.
- (Dough may be stored in refrigerator up to 4 days and baked as needed)
- Combine: 1 cup apricot preserves and 1 Tbl. lemon juice;
- mix to break up any large pieces of apricot.
- Divide: dough into four parts.
- Roll out one part on pastry cloth to a 14/12 inch rectangle.
- Spread with 1/4 cup of the apricot mixture.
- Sprinkle with one-fourth of the Nut filling
- Roll: tightly as for jelly roll, starting with the 14 inch side;
- seal edges.
- Place on ungreased baking sheet.
- Repeat process with remaining dough.
- Bake: in moderate oven (350) 30 to 40 minutes until golden brown.
- Cut in 1 inch slices
- Serve warm or cold, sprinkled with confectioners sugar.

NUT FILLING:

- Mix together 1 1/2 cups nuts, chopped (almonds, pecans or walnuts) 1 cup chopped coconut, 1/2 cup chopped raisins and 1/4 cup chopped maraschino cherries if desired.

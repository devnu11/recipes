## Ingredients

- 1/2 cup margarine or butter, softened
- 1 tablespoon snipped fresh basil or 1 teaspoon dried basil, crushed
- 1 tablespoon snipped parsley
- 2 teaspoons lemon juice
- 4 fish steaks, cut 1 inch thick (about 2 pounds total)*

## Method

- For margarine mixture, in a small mixing bowl combine margarine, basil, parsley, and lemon juice.
- Stir together till well blended.
- Place the fish steaks on a greased rack of an unheated broiler pan.
- Lightly brush fish with some of the margarine mixture.
- Broil 4 inches from the heat for 5 minutes.
- Using a wide spatula, carefully turn fish over.
- Lightly brush with more of the margarine mixture.
- Broil for 3 to 7 minutes more or till fish flakes easily when tested with a fork.
- To serve, spoon remaining margarine mixture on top of fish.

## Notes

Makes 4 servings.

*Fish steak suggestions: halibut, salmon, sea bass, shark, swordfish, tuna, whitefish.

Per serving: 444 calories, 46 g protein, 1 g carbohydrate, 28 g fat, 402 mg sodium.

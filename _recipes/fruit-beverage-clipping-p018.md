## Ingredients

- 1 1/2 cups frozen lightly sweetened red raspberries or unsweetened strawberries
- 1/2 of a 16-ounce package frozen unsweetened peach slices (2 cups)
- 1 6-ounce can frozen lemonade concentrate
- 2 medium bananas, sliced

## Method

- Thaw the raspberries, peaches, and lemonade concentrate.
- In a blender container combine raspberries, peaches, bananas, lemonade concentrate, and 1/2 cup cold water.
- Cover and blend till smooth.
- Sieve mixture and discard seeds, if desired.
- Transfer mixture to the Classic Sheer Go-Between Pitcher.
- Stir in 2 cups cold water.
- Serve immediately over ice in the 6-oz. Tumblers.
- Or, chill beverage in the refrigerator for several hours or overnight and stir just before serving.

## Notes

Makes about 8 (6-ounce) servings.

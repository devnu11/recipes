## Ingredients

- 3 Tablespoons McLean's Red Raspberry Vinegar
- 2/3 cup extra virgin olive oil
- 1 clove garlic, minced
- 1 teaspoon coarse salt

## Method

- Add McLean's Red Raspberry Vinegar to the minced garlic and salt.
- Stir vigorously and then whisk in olive oil.
- Refrigerate, although best served at room temperature.

## Ingredients

- 2 or 3 large bananas
- 2 cups sifted all purpose flour
- 1/2 tsp baking powder
- 3/4 tsp soda
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1/2 cup soft shortening
- 1 1/2 cups sugar
- 2 eggs (unbeaten)
- 1 tsp vanilla
- 1/4 cup sour milk (you can sour the milk by adding about 1 tsp vinegar)

## Method

- 1. Break into chunks 2 or 3 large bananas and mash until all big lumps are gone.
- Measure out one cup.
- 2. sift 2 cups sifted all purpose flour with 1/2 tsp baking powder, 3/4 tsp soda and 1/2 tsp salt.
- 3. Put in large bowl 1/2 cup soft shortening, 1 1/2 cups sugar, 2 eggs (unbeaten) and 1 tsp vanilla.
- Beat until smooth.
- Add 1/4 cup sour milk (you can sour the milk by adding about 1 tsp vinegar) and the sifted flour mixture,
- Alternately with the mashed bananas and beat until smooth.
- Bake at 350 for 30-35 minutes.

## Notes

This recipe is for 9" cake pans or the one layer 13 1/2 by 9 pan

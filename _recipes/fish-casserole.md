## Ingredients

- 2 lbs. fish fillets
- 1 cup bread crumbs (not dry from can)
- 1 tablespoon vinegar
- 1 tablespoon lemon juice
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1/2 cup melted butter
- 1 1/4 teaspoons prepared mustard
- 1/2 teaspoon pepper

## Method

- Sprinkle crumbs on bottom of 1 1/2 qt casserole dish.
- Put fish on top of crumbs.
- Make sauce with other ingredients and pour over fish
- (Do not cook sauce before adding to fish.)
- Bake at 400° for 20 minutes, covered.
- Baste occasionally.

## Notes

Serves 6.

## Ingredients

- (1) Noodles - Chinese
- (2) Rice
- (3) Chicken
- (4) Gravy
- (5) Celery - cut small
- (6) green onions - cut small
- (7) sliced tomatoes
- (8) gravy
- (9) grated cheddar cheese
- (10) drained pineapple, crushed
- (11) Coconut
- (12) slivered almonds
- (13) Cherries, marachino

## Method

- Fry chicken, very little seasoning.
- when cool - cut in strips
- make plenty of gravy

## Notes

It is important that you serve as suggested -

Ingredients are numbered 1-13 in circles in the original (serving order).

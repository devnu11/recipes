## Ingredients

- 6 oz (3 1/2 cups) medium noodles
- 1 6 1/2-7 or 9 1/4 oz can tuna
- 1/2 cup mayonnaise'
- 1 cup sliced celery
- 1/3 cup chopped onion
- 1/2 cup diced green pepper
- 1/2 cup chopped pimiento
- 1 tsp. salt
- 1 can condensed cream of celery soup
- 1/2 cup milk
- 1 cup shredded sharp process cheese
- 1/2 cup slivered blanched almonds toasted(optional)

## Method

- Cook nooldes in boiling water, salted, till tender.
- drain.
- combine noodles, drained tuna, mayonnaise, vegetables, and salt.
- Blend soup and milk
- heat through.
- Add cheese
- heat and stir until cheese melts.
- Add to noodle mixture.
- Turn into 1 1/2 quart casserole.
- Top with almonds.
- Bake in hot oven (425) about 20 minutes.
- Trim with lemon wedges and water cress.

## Notes

Makes 6 servings

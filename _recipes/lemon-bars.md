## Ingredients

- 1 cup all purpose flour
- 1/4 cup sifted powdered sugar
- 1/2 cup butter or margarine
- 2 eggs
- 3/4 cup granulated sugar
- 1/2 tsp finely shredded lemon peel
- 3 Tbl lemon juice
- 2 Tbl all purpose flour
- 1/4 tsp baking powder

## Method

- Sift together 1 cup flour and 1/4 cup powdered sugar
- cut in butter till mixture clings together.
- Pat into ungreased 8x8x2" baking pan.
- Bake at 350° for 10 or 12 min.
- In mixer bowl beat eggs
- add granulated sugar, lemon peel & juice.
- Beat till slightly thick & smooth, 8 to 10 min.
- Stir together 2 Tbl flour and baking powder
- add to egg mixture.
- Blend just till moistened.
- Pour over baked layer.
- Bake at 350° for 20 to 25 Min.
- Sift more powdered sugar over top.
- Cool.
- Cut into bars

## Notes

Makes 20

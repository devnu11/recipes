## Ingredients

- 1/2 c margarine
- 1 c brown sugar
- 1/4 c. light Karo[?] Syrup
- dash salt
- 1 tsp. soda
- 3 qts. popped popcorn (2/3 to 1 c. unpopped)

## Method

- In glass container combine margarine, brown sugar, & Karo
- Microwave on high 5 min stirring half way thru time.
- Stir in soda
- popped corn in brown paper bag
- Pour[?] brownsugar mix over popcorn.
- Put in microwave cook on high for 1 min.
- Stir[?] cook on hi for 1 min more

## Ingredients

- 3 c diced[?] Rhubarb
- 2 c strawberries
- 1 3/4 c sugar
- 1/2 t salt
- 1/4 c flour
- 3 eggs
- (3 T toasted bread crumbs)
- 2 T soft butter

## Method

- Mix sugar, salt, flour and add slightly beaten eggs and finally, fruit
- 2 crusted pie - dot with butter
- 450° 20 min 350° 30 min

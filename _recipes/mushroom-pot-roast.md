## Ingredients

- 3 to 4 lb beef pot roast
- 2 onions sliced
- 1/2 cup water
- 1/4 cup catsup
- 1/3 cup cooking sherry
- 1 clove garlic, minced
- 1/4 tsp each dry mustard, majoram, rosemary, thyme
- 1 bay leaf
- 1 6oz can broiled sliced mushrooms

## Method

- Trim off excess fat.
- Dredge meat in flour.
- Brown slowly on all sides in a little hot fat.
- Season generously with salt and pepper.
- Add onions.
- Mix and add remaining ingredients, except mushroom[?]
- Cook slowly 2 1/2 hrs or till done.
- Add mushrooms and liquid and heat.
- Make gravy.

## Notes

6 to 8 servings

## Ingredients

- 8 oz cream cheese
- 1/2 Cup sour cream
- 1 or 2 egg
- 1/4[?] cup powdered sugar

## Method

- Mix all ingredients with a hand mixer.
- cook in microwave 2-3 minutes
- beat again repeat 3-4 times.
- put mixture in crepe & add a little butter to the pan.
- fry each Blintz

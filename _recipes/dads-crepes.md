## Ingredients

- 1 cup flour
- ~1 cup milk (1/2 can be buttermilk)
- 1 egg
- 1 T sugar (for dessert crêpes)
- 1 T oil
- 1/2 tsp. vanilla[?] (optional)

## Method

- cook in a buttered pan.
- add more butter if the stick
- add more milk if unable to spread

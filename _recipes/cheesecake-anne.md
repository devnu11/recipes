## Ingredients

- 3 8 oz pkg cream cheese
- 6 eggs separated
- 1 cup sugar
- 2 Tbl. flour
- 1 cup commercial sour cream
- 1 tsp lemon juice
- 1 tsp vanilla

## Method

- Beat cream cheese with egg yolks until smooth.
- Beat in sugar, flour, sour cream, lemon juice and vanilla.
- Whip egg whites until they hold short distinct peaks then fold gently into cheese mixture.
- Pour batter into an ungreased 8 or 9 inch pan (one with removeable bottom)
- Bake in 325° oven for 1 hour & 10 min or until center no longer appears soft.
- Let cool at room temp at least 2 hrs before cutting or chill before serving if you prefer a creamier consistency.

## Notes

Recipe from: Mom

Serves: 12

## Ingredients

- 1/4 cup chopped green pepper
- 1 cup mushrooms, sliced
- 2 Tbl flour
- 2 Tbl butter
- 3/4 teasp. salt
- 2 cups light cream
- 3 cups cooked chicken
- 3 egg yolks
- 1/2 teasp paprika
- 1/4 cup soft butter
- 1 teasp onion juice ("lemon" crossed out, "ONION" written above)
- 1 Tbl. lemon Juice
- 2 Tbl. sherry
- 1/4 cup diced pimiento

## Method

- Cook green pepper & mushrooms in 2 Tbl. butter until tender but not brown
- push veg. one side and blend flour and salt into the butter.
- Gradually stir in cream cook & stir until sauce thickens.
- Add chicken & heat thoroughly, stirring occasionally.
- Meanwhile in a small bowl, blend egg yolks paprika & 1/4 cup soft butter, set aside.
- To chicken mixture add onion juice, lemon juice, and sherry.
- Be sure chicken is bubbling, then add yolk mixture all at once stirring until blended.
- Add pimiento.

## Notes

To transfer to a chafing dish remove saucepan from heat just before blending in yolk mixture.

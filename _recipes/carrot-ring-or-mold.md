## Ingredients

- 2 1/2 cups mashed cooked carrots
- 2 Tbl onion juice or minced onion
- 2 Tbl melted butter
- 2 eggs, well beaten
- 1 Tbl flour
- 1 cup top milk or cream
- Season with salt, pepper, paprika to taste

## Method

- Mix.
- Pack into a buttered 1 qt ring mold.
- Set in a shallow pan of hot water.
- Baked at 350 until firm.
- 40 to 50 minutes

## Ingredients

- 4# lean country style spareribs
- 1/2 cup each soy sauce & chili sauce
- 2 Tbls each honey and sherry
- 2 cloves garlic, crushed
- 2 green onions, chopped
- 1/2 tsp. salt

## Method

- Arrange the spareribs in one layer in a baking pan.
- Combine the soy sauce, chili sauce, honey, sherry, crushed garlic, chopped onions and salt.
- Brush the meat generously on all sides with the sauce.
- Let the meat marinate 2 to 3 hours, turning several times.
- Then turn the meat fat side up and bake in 350° oven for 1 1/2 hours.
- Baste the meat occasionally with the sauce.

## Notes

Makes about 6 servings

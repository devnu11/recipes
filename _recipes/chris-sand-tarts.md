## Ingredients

- Cream:
    - 2 cups butter
    - 1/4 tsp salt
    - 2 1/2 cups sugar
- Add:
    - 2 eggs
- Add:
    - 4 cups sifted flour

## Method

- Chill overnight.
- Roll out thin.
- Cut with cookie cutter.
- Brush slightly with beaten egg white
- sprinkle with cinnamon and sugar.
- Decorate with piece of walnut.
- Bake at 350 about 10 minutes.

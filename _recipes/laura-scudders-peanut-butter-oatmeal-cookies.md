## Ingredients

- 3/4 cup butter or margarine, softened
- 1 cup firmly packed light brown sugar
- 1 cup Laura Scudder's® Peanut Butter, at room temperature
- 2 eggs
- 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
- 1/4 teaspoon salt, optional
- 1 cup unsifted flour
- 1 cup quick-cooking oats
- 1 teaspoon baking soda.

## Method

- In large mixer bowl, beat butter and sugar until well blended.
- Add peanut butter, eggs, vanilla and salt if desired
- beat well.
- Add flour, oats and baking soda
- mix well.
- Chill until firm, 6 hours or overnight.
- Roll into 1-inch balls
- place 2 inches apart on ungreased baking sheets.
- Flatten with fork or glass dipped in sugar.
- Bake in preheated 375° oven 9 to 10 minutes or until golden.
- Let stand 2 to 3 minutes
- remove from baking sheet.
- Store covered at room temperature.

## Notes

(Makes about 4 dozen)

Peanut Butter Cookies: Omit quick-cooking oats. Increase flour to 2 cups. Proceed as above.

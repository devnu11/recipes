## Ingredients

- Cream together:
    - 2 c butter
    - 2 c sugar
    - 2 c brown sugar
- Add:
    - 4 eggs
    - 2 t vanilla
- Mix together in seperate bowl:
    - 4 c flour
    - 5 cups oatmeal (put small amounts in blender until it turns to powder) measure before powdered
    - 1 t salt
    - 2 t baking powder
    - 2 t baking soda

## Method

- Mix all of the above
- Add: 24 oz choc. chips and 1 finely grated plain hershey bar
- Add: 3 c chopped nuts

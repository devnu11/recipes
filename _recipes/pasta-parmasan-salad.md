## Ingredients

- 4 cups uncooked pasta (veggie rotelle[?])
- 1/4 c. pesto sauce
- 1/4 c. olive oil
- 2 tomatoes chopped
- 1/4 c. green onion finely chopped
- 1/2 c. olives chopped
- 1/2 c. parmasan cheese
- 1 t garlic powder
- 2 t wine vinegar
- 10 oz pkg froz. spinach - chopped or 1 1/2 fresh spinach, cooked & chopped

## Method

- Cook pasta and combine rest of ingrediants.

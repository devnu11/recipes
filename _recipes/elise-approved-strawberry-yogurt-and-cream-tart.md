## The short bread crust

- 1/2 c plus 2 T of butter (one stick plus 1/4) regular salted butter melted (I used the microwave for 45 seconds)
- 11/4 c all-purpose flour
- 1/3 c confectionary sugar
- 1/2 t salt

- Mix the dry ingredients and pour in the melted better.
- Mix well into a paste.
- Spread into a crust in a 10 inch tart pan (a tart pan has a bottom that lifts out has shown in insert).
- Be sure to reach the top of the sides.
- Avoid too much dough along the edge.
- Bake for 20 minutes at 350 degrees F.
- It should be golden brown.
- Allow it to cool.
- Do NOT remove outer ring.

## The cream filling

- 1 c of heavy cream whipped to stiff peaks (avoid making butter)
- 1 c of plain whole milk yogurt (do not try with nonfat or low fat, you will be disappointed)
- 1/2 c strawberry jam or preserves divided
    - half spread over the bottom of the cooled crust
    - the rest folded into the whipped cream with the yogurt

- Spread half of the strawberry preserves over the bottom of the cooled crust.
- Be careful not to tear the crust.
- Fold the yogurt and the rest of the preserves into the whipped cream.
- Do NOT over mix.
- Fill the crust and gently push the mixture to the edges of the crust.
- It will be slightly mounted in the middle.
- You can pause here and refrigerate or continue to at the fruit topping as your time allows.

## The fruit topping

- The best effect was achieved by paving the top of the filling with about 1 pound of halved strawberries (one small basket or package).
- Sliced berries and standing whole berries were also found to be satisfactory but were less appealing in appearance.
- The tart can be served immediately but allowing it to set in the refrigerator for several hours is a plus.
- Over night is even better.
- However, overnight is not recommended as the crust tends to absorb moisture.
- List the tart from the ring for serving exposing the crust and making it easier to serve.

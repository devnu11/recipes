## Ingredients

- 1/2 c butter or margarine
- 1 c brown sugar
- 1/4 c milk (cream is better)

## Method

- Melt butter.
- Add sugar and boil 2 min.
- Add milk and bring to a boil.
- Cool
- Add powdered sugar and beat until smooth and correct consistency.

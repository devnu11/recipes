## Ingredients

- 1 2 lb flank steak
- seasoned instant meat tenderizer
- 1/4 tsp seasoned pepper
- 6 bacon strips
- 1 tomato, cut in 6 wedges
- 1/4 cup bottled oil and vinegar dressing
- 2 Tbl butter or margarine
- 6 stemmed mushroom caps

## Method

- About 1 hour before serving
- Preheat broiler 10 minutes or as manufacturer directs.
- Then trim excess fat from steak
- cuts off thin tail with sharp knife and score one side of it in a diamond pattern.
- Prepare steak with meat tenderizer then sprinkle with seasoned pepper.
- Starting at long side of steak roll it up, then wrap bacon strips around roll, securing each with toothpicks.
- Next slice roll between bacon strips.
- Broil mignonettes, cut side down, 8 minutes on one side and 5 minutes on the other, or to desired rareness.
- If bacon is not crisp enough stand mignonettes on edge and broils them a minute or so longer, turing them as they brown.
- Meanwhile, refrigerat tomato wedges in dressing, in a small bowl.
- Then inhot butter, in skillet, saute mushrooms till golden - about 5 minutes.
- When done, arrange mignonettes, cut side down, on platter, tops each with a mushroom cap, and garnishes the platter with tomato wedges.

## Notes

Makes 6 servings.

Serve the mignonettes with peas and onions, a crisp green salad, refrigerated dinner rolls, a[nd?] lemon ice milk topped with strawberries.

## Ingredients

- 2c sifted flour
- 1T baking powder
- 2t salt
- 1t cayenne pepper
- 2 eggs, lightly beaten
- 1 12oz bottle dark beer
- 2 pounds lingcod.
- fat for deep frying

## Method

- Place dry ingredients & eggs in bowl & mix well, then add beer while mixing constantly
- use as few strokes as necessary if batter used right away.
- If the batter can rest 2 hour in refrig. beat to smooth consistency.
- Throughly pat dry fish & dip in batter
- fry in 350° fat turning once till nicely browned.
- Do not crowded in fryer.
- Allow oil to reheat between batches

## Notes

Serves 4-6

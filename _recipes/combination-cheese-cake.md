## Ingredients

- Crust for Combination Cheese Cake
    - 1 1/2 cups lightly toasted bread crumbs (or zwieback)
    - 1/3 cup granulated sugar
    - 1/4 teaspoon vanilla
    - 6 tablespoons butter, first measured and then melted
- Filling for Combination Cheese Cake
    - 1/2 pound bakers' dry cottage cheese
    - 1/2 pound cream cheese
    - 1/4 cup egg yolk
    - 1/4 teaspoon salt
    - 1 teaspoon vanilla
    - 1/4 cup coffee cream
    - 1/2 cup granulated sugar
    - 1/2 cup egg whites beaten until they cling to bowl, then add 1/3 cup granulated sugar little at a time and continue to beat another minute at high speed after all the sugar has been added

## Method

- Light oven and set at 375° F.
- Combine all ingredients for crust, blend with a fork, then with fingers.
- Grease bottom and sides (halfway) on a loose-bottom pan that measures about 3 x 9, with 1 teaspoon soft shortening.
- Cover sides of pan with about 1/2 cup of the crumb mixture.
- Reserve 3/4 cupful for top of cake.
- Pour rest of crumb mixture (which is about 1 1/4 cupful) into bottom of pan.
- Spread it as evenly as possible, then pat crumbs solidly, using a large spoon or with a piece of wax paper to prevent crumbs from sticking to fingers.
- Keep crust in coldest part of refrigerator while preparing filling.
- It is necessary to have crust quite firm before pouring filling into it.
- Combine the cheeses, egg yolks, salt, vanilla, and cream in a 9- or 10-inch bowl.
- With electric beater or a heavy spoon beat until creamy and smooth, then add sugar a little at a time and continue to beat only until smooth.
- Takes about 2 minutes with electric beater, a little longer with a spoon.
- In a No. 7 bowl, beat egg whites until they cling to bowl, then add 1/3 cup granulated sugar (this is in addition to the 1/2 cup used in the cheese mixture).
- Beat 1 minute at high speed after all sugar has been added.

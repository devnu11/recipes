## Ingredients

- 2 c flour
- 2 t salt
- 2 t sugar
- 2 T yeast

## Method

- Mix with fork.
- Add 2 c. very hot tap water.
- Mix set for 10 min.
- Add ~2 1/2 C flour
- Knead 1-2 min or till doesn't stick
- Put 2 T oil in bowl.
- Turn dough over once
- Rise 30 min - 1 hour.
- grease or cornmeal pan
- bake 425° for 20 min.

## Notes

makes 2-3 crusts

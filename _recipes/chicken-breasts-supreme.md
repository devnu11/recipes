## Ingredients

- 3 breasts boned and halved
- salt
- 7 Tbl butter
    - 5 tbl for cooking chicken
    - 2 remaining Tbl. for sauce
- 2 Tbl flour
- 1/4 tsp salt
- white pepper
- 1/2 cup chicken broth
- 1/4 cup white wine
- 1/4 cup heavy cream

## Method

- Sprinkle breasts with salt.
- Heat 5 tbl of the butter in a 12" skillet.
- Add chicken and cook 3 min. over medium heat and turn over and cook 3 min. or until done.
- In a separate saucepan melt the 2 remaining Tbl. of butter.
- Blend in flour, salt and pepper.
- Stir in stock and cook over medium heat, stirring until thickened.
- Add wine and simmer 2 minutes.
- Just before serving stir in heavy cream and heat.
- Pour sauce over breasts.

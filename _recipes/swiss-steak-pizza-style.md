## Ingredients

- 1 1/2 lbs round steak, 1 inch thick
- 2 Tbl. flour
- 1 tsp garlic salt
- 1/8 tsp. pepper
- 1/2 tsp oregano
- 1 onion, sliced
- 1/2 cup Burgundy, or other red wine
- 1 1 lb can stewed tomatoes
- 2 Tbl chopped parsley
- 4 Jack or cheddar cheese wedges

## Method

- Trim any excess fat from meat.
- Mix flour, garlic salt, pepper and oregano together.
- Pound seasoned flour into meat.
- Cut steak into 4 serving pieces
- Brown slowly and well on both sides in heated fat using a heavy skillet or Dutch oven.
- Add onions during the last 5 minutes of browning.
- cook until softened.
- Add wine and tomatoes.
- Cover and cook slowly until meat is almost fork tender, about 1 1/2 hours.
- Or, bake in a moderate oven (350)
- Add undrained mushrooms and parsley
- cook 10 or 15 minutes longer.
- Skim any fat from gravy before serving.
- Gravy may be thickened with a little cornstarch mixed with cold water, if desired.
- Taste and add a little more salt if necessary.
- Arrange meat on heated serving dish
- top each piece with a slice or triangle of jack or cheddar cheese.

## Notes

Serves 4

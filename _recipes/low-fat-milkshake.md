## Ingredients

- 2 bannas
- 1 cup frozen berries
- 1 1/2 cup milk
- 1 tespoon sugar
- 5 ice cupes[?]
- 1/2 cup nonfat yogurt

## Method

- Put everything but ice cubes in blendor, mix.
- When mixed add ice cubes.

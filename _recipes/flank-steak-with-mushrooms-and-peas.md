## Method

- Slice 1/2 lb mushrooms (you should have about 3 cups)
- cut celery in 1/4 inch diagonal slices to make 1 cup.
- Cut 1 1/4 lbs flank steak in half lengthwise
- slice each half, on the diagonal, into 1/4 inch thick strips.
- Place mushrooms, celery, and 1 pkg (10 oz) frozen peas in a large heavy frying pan thas has a cover.
- Add 1 cup water.
- Cover, bring to a boil, and cook over medium high heat for about 5 mins. or until celery is tender but still crisp.
- Drain, reserving liquid, and place vegetables in an uncovered bowl.
- Gradually add 1/2 cup water to 1 Tbl cornstarch to blend smoothly, stir in 2 Tbl dry Sherry. 1 1/2 Tbl soy sauce, 2 tsp grated fresh ginger (or 1/2 tsp ground ginger), and 1/4 tsp garlic powder.
- Brown meat quickly in 3 Tbl. very hot salad oil, reduce heat, return vegetables to pan with soy sauce mixture, and cook for about 1 min. longer, stirring constantly, until sauce is thickened.
- Blend in about 1/3 cup of the reserved vegetable liquid, if you prefer a thinner sauce.
- Serve with steamed rice or Chinese noodles.

## Notes

Rice or thin Chinese noodles complement the Oriental flavors of this entree.

Makes 4 servings.

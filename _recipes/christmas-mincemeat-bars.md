## Ingredients

- cream:
    - 1 1/2 c brown sugar
    - 2 eggs
    - 2 T molasses
    - 1 T soft butter
    - 1 t vanilla
- add to above:
    - 2 c flour
    - 1/2 t salt & soda
    - 1 t cinnamon & cloves
- 3 T hot water
- 1/4 c raisins
- 1/4 c slivered almonds
- 9 oz mincemeat - broken up w/ fork

## Method

- Bake in greased 9x13 pan for 12-15 min.
- Put icing on immediately with
    - 1 1/2 c confectioner's sugar
    - 3 T hot milk
    - 1/2 t vanilla & almond extract

## Notes

Loretta

400° 12-15 min

makes 6 doz 2 x 1 1/2 bars.

## Ingredients

- 3 large chicken breasts, cut in half
- salt and pepper
- 1/4 cup butter or margarine
- 1 can condensed cream of chicken soup
- 3/4 cup cooking sauterne
- 1 5 oz can (2/3) cup water chestnuts, drained and sliced
- 1 3 oz can 2/3 cup broiled sliced mushrooms, drained[?]
- 2 Tbl chopped green pepper
- 1/4 tsp crushed thyme

## Method

- Lightly season chicken with salt and pepper;
- brown slowly in butter in skillet.
- Arrange browned chicken skin side up in 11 1/2x7 1/2x1 1/2 inch baking pan.
- For the sauce, add soup to drippings in skillet;
- slowly add sauterne, stirring smooth.
- Add remaining ingredients;
- heat to boiling.
- Pour sauce over chicken.
- Cover with foil and bake in 350 oven 25 minutes.
- Uncover; continue baking 25 to 35 min or till chicken is tender.
- Serve with hot fluffy rice, if desired.
